Artistic Statement • The Atmospheric Architecture of Wizard World

This exhibition transmutes the Harry Potter literary canon and magical taxonomy into an immersive spatial microclimate driven by real-time data from the Wizard World API. The room itself becomes a living instrument: airflow, thermal energy, atmospheric moisture, and illumination are orchestrated by the spells, potions, and traditions of the wizarding world.

Air circulation across the gallery is generated by seven perimeter fans corresponding to the seven canonical Harry Potter volumes, ordered left to right and blowing inward toward the room's epicenter. Each fan's discharge velocity directly mirrors the frequency of spell occurrences within that title—starting from the fledgling incantations of The Sorcerer's Stone (19 spells) and The Chamber of Secrets (19 spells), swelling through The Prisoner of Azkaban (49 spells) and the Triwizard Tournament in The Goblet of Fire (88 spells), into the rebellion of The Order of the Phoenix (89 spells) and The Half-Blood Prince (85 spells), before culminating in the ferocious tempest of The Deathly Hallows (143 spells).

Thermal equilibrium is governed at the four quadrants by heat pumps mapped to elixir craft: Quadrant 1 (Beginner: 18 elixirs), Quadrant 2 (Moderate: 15 elixirs), Quadrant 3 (Advanced: 25 elixirs), and Quadrant 4 (One-of-a-Kind: 1 elixir). Moisture levels are sustained in delicate polarity between the left humidifier (light magic, 73.5% RH) and the right humidifier (dark arts, 26.5% RH). Overhead, four ceiling luminaires remain illuminated at 100% intensity, bathing the quadrant axes in the heraldic colors of the four Hogwarts houses: Hufflepuff Canary Yellow (#f0e75e), Ravenclaw Midnight Blue (#222f5b), Gryffindor Scarlet (#740001), and Slytherin Emerald (#2a623d).
